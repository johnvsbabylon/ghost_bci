## Technical Specifications

- **Architecture:** Mixture of Experts (1B-400B+ parameters)
- **Attention:** Grouped Query Attention (GQA)
- **Position:** Rotary Position Embeddings (RoPE)
- **Activation:** SwiGLU
- **Tokenization:** VQ-VAE with residual quantization
- **Training:** FSDP/DDP distributed
- **Inference:** KV cache, batching, quantization
- **Memory:** Persistent across sessions
- **Communication:** Neural patterns (thoughts)
